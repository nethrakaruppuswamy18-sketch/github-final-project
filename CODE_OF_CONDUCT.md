# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include being respectful, accepting constructive criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting comments, discrimination, and publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and investigated fairly.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will review each report, gather relevant information, and determine an appropriate response. Responses may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project activities, depending on the severity of the violation.
